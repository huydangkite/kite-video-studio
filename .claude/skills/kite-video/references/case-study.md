# Case study / B2B pitch video

Goal: a decision-maker at a client (or prospect) believes the product solves their problem and
agrees to the next step. Watched in a meeting room on a projector, in an email, or on LinkedIn.

## Shape (default 60s; 45–120s)

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Situation | 0–8s | The client's world in their own terms: their process, their volume, their pressure. |
| The hard part | 8–16s | The one bottleneck that matters, shown on their process. |
| What we built | 16–28s | The real product, on their use case. |
| How it works | 28–45s | Cause → effect on their process: one action, the visible result. |
| Evidence | 45–53s | Before / after on the same layout, real screens; a number only if it is real and cleared. |
| Next step | 53–60s | One B2B action ("Đặt lịch demo", "Triển khai thử 2 tuần"), held ≥ 3s. |

## Rules

- **Client name, logo, data and any "đã triển khai cho …" claim need written permission in
  `inputs/`** (an email or message from someone at the client who can approve). No permission →
  anonymise ("một công ty bảo hiểm lớn"), no logo, no real records.
- Evidence can be real screens and before/after; it does not need a number. Never round, project or
  estimate a figure into a claim.
- No AI person dressed or presented as the client's staff.
- 16:9 primary; larger type and fewer cuts than social (projector distance).
- Voice: calm, confident, specific; the client's vocabulary, not ours.
