## Tone of voice

- Vietnamese: gần gũi, rõ ràng, không phóng đại. Xưng "bạn".
- English: friendly, direct, no hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing").

## Always / never

- Always end with the logo and one call to action.
- Never show real customer data; use the demo account.
- Never mention competitors by name.
